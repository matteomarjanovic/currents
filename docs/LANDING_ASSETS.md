# Currents landing page imagery

The hero and product previews use stock photographs and one historical print from PxHere. Their source listings identify the work as CC0 and record upload dates from 2016 to 2018. The closing photograph is from Pexels. The WebP files are crops adapted for the homepage. [PxHere terms](https://pxhere.com/en/terms) · [Creative Commons CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) · [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/)

Tiles are 360 × 360 px. The hero and product previews crop those tiles at different ratios. The closing image is 1440 × 960 px with a 720 × 480 px derivative. Keep the water texture distinct around the closing copy. The Organize mockup shows the selected save in both the grid and its details pane.

Use different photographs across sections. For color search, keep indigo, vermilion, and moss distinct across subjects. The semantic search results depict warm indoor light, related images share fern shapes, and library search shows blue textile textures.

## Atmosphere icons

The Atmosphere illustration in `frontend/src/lib/components/landing/atmosphere.svelte` uses the existing Currents mark and locally stored 128 × 128 WebP app icons in `frontend/static/landing/atmosphere/`. The Offprint icon comes from its public site icon. The Bluesky, Grain, Leaflet, Margin, pckt, Popfeed, and Tangled icons come from the public app illustration on Offprint’s homepage. The `pckt.webp` source is named `pcktblog.jpg` there. [Offprint icon](https://offprint.app/favicon.svg) · [Offprint homepage](https://offprint.app/)

Spark uses its Android launcher icon. Sifa ID uses its touch icon on a light backing for contrast. Semble uses its web app icon, mu uses the square pink avatar (`mu-logos_mu_avatar_pink-18.png`) from its official brand pack, and Surf uses the icon from its Google Play listing. [Spark](https://github.com/sprksocial/client/blob/main/android/app/src/main/ic_launcher-playstore.png) · [Sifa ID](https://sifa.id/apple-icon.png) · [Semble](https://semble.so/semble-icon-192x192.png) · [mu brand pack](https://hello.mu.social/goodies/) · [Surf](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.flipboard.surf)
